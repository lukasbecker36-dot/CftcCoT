# Eris SOFR Swap Futures — 2-Year Trend Analysis (Mar 2024 – Mar 2026)

## 10-Year Eris SOFR Swap

**Open Interest: 80k → 222k (+176%)**

The 10Y has been the most established Eris tenor and shows a steady, sustained build in OI over two years. The story unfolds in three phases:

- **Mar–Sep 2024**: Lev money was already net short and growing (~-24k → -69k), while dealers and asset managers were near-flat. OI grew from 80k to 129k. Hedge funds were building a "higher for longer" rates position.
- **Oct 2024–Jun 2025**: A consolidation phase. OI fluctuated 100k–140k. Lev money shorts stayed in the -44k to -70k range. The big shift was **dealers going from -2.5k to +27k net long** (Jul 2025), coinciding with new "Other Reportable" flows entering (~+11k). This looks like a structural change in market-making.
- **Jul 2025–Mar 2026**: The acceleration phase. OI exploded to 222k. **Lev money shorts doubled** from -70k to -153k. **Asset managers finally entered**, going from near-zero to +22k net long. Dealers stabilized around +25-28k.

**Concentration**: Top-4 short concentration fell from 99% to 82% — the market has diversified on the short side but remains dominated by a handful of players.

## 5-Year Eris SOFR Swap

**Open Interest: 41k → 185k (+355%)**

The 5Y shows the most dramatic transformation:

- **Mar–Nov 2024**: Classic build phase. OI rose from 41k to 99k. Lev money went from -14k to -68k net short. Dealers fluctuated between +7k and -3k. "Other Reportable" grew to ~10k net long. Asset managers were essentially absent (near zero).
- **Dec 2024–Jan 2025**: A **sharp deleveraging event**. OI collapsed from 99k to 44k (55% drop in ~2 months). Lev money shorts fell from -68k to -9k. This coincides with year-end position squaring and likely reflects the volatility around the Dec 2024 Fed meeting.
- **Feb 2025–Mar 2026**: Rebuild and new regime. OI surged back to 185k (4.5x the Jan low). The big new development: **asset managers swung from -28k short to +17k long** (a 45k swing in ~3 months, starting Dec 2025). Lev money rebuilt shorts to -61k. A new "Other Reportable" contingent has taken root at ~21k net long.

**Concentration**: Top-4 long concentration rose from 55% to 72% — the long side is getting more concentrated even as OI grows, meaning a few big players are driving the flows.

## 2-Year Eris SOFR Swap

**Open Interest: 21k → 120k (+483%)**

- **Mar 2024–May 2025**: A slow, steady build. OI grew from 21k to 51k. Lev money was modestly net long (+5k to +14k) — the opposite of longer tenors. "Other Reportable" was the dominant category (+7k to +16k).
- **Jun 2025**: **Lev money flipped short** for the first time (-7.6k), marking a regime change.
- **Dec 2025**: **Asset managers made a dramatic entrance**, going from near-zero to +35k net long in a single week (likely a large block trade). This is the most abrupt positioning shift in any Eris tenor.
- **Jan–Mar 2026**: Asset managers continued building to +51k. Lev money deepened shorts to -31k. OI surged to 120k.

**Concentration**: The 2Y has extreme concentration: **98% of longs held by top 4 traders** throughout the entire 2-year period. This is essentially a bilateral market between a few very large accounts.

## 3-Year Eris SOFR Swap (new — Sep 2025 onward)

**Open Interest: 15k → 114k (from near-zero in 6 months)**

This tenor barely existed before Sep 2025 and exploded to 114k OI. The positioning is stark:
- **Asset managers: +80k net long** (100% of longs held by top 4)
- **Lev money: -58k net short**
- **Dealers: -15k net short** (absorbing asset manager flow)

This is the purest expression of the "asset managers receiving fixed vs lev money paying fixed" theme.

---

## Cross-Tenor Themes

**1. Regime change in late 2025**: Across all tenors, something fundamental shifted in Nov–Dec 2025. Asset managers went from absent/short to massively long. This likely reflects a conviction call that the Fed cutting cycle would accelerate, or that term premium would compress.

**2. The lev money / asset manager divide is unprecedented**: The scale of the asset manager long vs lev money short divergence across the entire curve is the defining feature of the past 6 months. It suggests a fundamental disagreement between institutional investors (rates falling) and hedge funds (rates staying elevated or rising).

**3. Year-end 2024 deleveraging was a warning**: The 5Y OI halved in Dec 2024. With positions now 2-4x larger, a similar year-end squeeze in Dec 2026 could be much more impactful.

**4. Market structure concern**: Concentration ratios are extreme (82-100% for top 4) across all tenors. This market has grown rapidly but remains thinly held. Any large unwind — especially from the asset manager side — would face significant liquidity challenges.

**5. Eris as the new swap futures**: Total OI across all 4 tenors has grown from ~140k (Mar 2024) to ~640k+ (Mar 2026), a 4.5x increase. Eris SOFR swaps are rapidly becoming a significant venue for rates positioning, likely taking share from OTC swaps.

# Eris vs SOFR Futures vs Treasury Futures — Comparative Analysis (Mar 2024 – Mar 2026)

## The Common Theme: Asset Managers Long, Lev Money Short

The asset-manager-long / leveraged-money-short dynamic seen in Eris is **not unique** — it's the dominant positioning theme across the entire US rates complex. But the scale, timing, and nuances differ significantly.

## Key Differences

### 1. SOFR-3M: The most dramatic flip in all of rates

SOFR-3M futures saw the biggest positioning reversal anywhere. Asset managers went from **-1M net short** (Mar 2024, positioned for higher-for-longer) to **+1M net long** (mid-2025, positioned for cuts), before pulling back to near-flat recently. Leveraged money did the mirror image: from +700k long to -876k short.

The timing is revealing: the asset manager flip into net long happened progressively through H1 2024, well before the Sep 2024 Fed cut. By the time Eris asset managers started buying in late 2025, the SOFR-3M trade had already been on for over a year.

### 2. Treasury Notes: The persistent, grinding build

Unlike the volatile SOFR-3M positioning, Treasury note futures (2Y, 5Y, 10Y) show a slow, steady, one-directional build:
- Asset managers have been **adding longs consistently** for 2 years, with no major reversals
- Lev money has been steadily adding shorts
- The 5Y is the epicenter: asset managers hold +2.86M net long, the largest position in any single contract

This looks like a structural, multi-year bet on rate normalization, not a tactical trade.

### 3. The long bond is the outlier

UST Bond (30Y) is the only contract where the asset-manager/lev-money dynamic **didn't** intensify. Asset managers added only +36k (vs +1.37M in 2Y Notes). Lev money actually *reduced* shorts by 70k. This suggests conviction on rate cuts is concentrated in the front end and belly — the market is NOT positioning for a bull flattener. If anything, it implies participants expect the long end to be anchored by term premium or supply concerns.

### 4. Eris is a magnified, late-cycle version of the same trade

Eris SOFR swaps show the same directional positioning as Treasury futures but with three distinguishing features:

- **Timing**: The Eris asset manager build started in late 2025 — roughly 18 months after the same trade was initiated in SOFR-3M and Treasury futures. Eris is the *late mover*.
- **Growth**: OI growth of 175-483% dwarfs Treasury futures (12-32%). Eris is still in a rapid adoption phase.
- **Concentration**: Top-4 concentration of 82-100% in Eris vs 17-31% in Treasuries. The Treasury market has broad-based participation; Eris is a handful of large accounts.

### 5. Dealer positioning tells different stories

- **Treasury futures**: Dealers are consistently net short across all tenors (absorbing the asset manager flow). This is textbook dealer intermediation.
- **SOFR-3M**: Dealers swing between large positive and negative positions — more of a directional participant than intermediary.
- **Eris 10Y**: Dealers are +28k net long — unusually, they're on the *same side* as asset managers. This suggests dealers may be using Eris to hedge OTC swap books rather than purely market-making.

## The Big Picture

The entire US rates market is positioned for lower rates, but the bet is structured differently across venues:

- **SOFR-3M** → short-term rate expectations (Fed policy path). The trade has already partially unwound — asset managers pulled back from +1M to near-flat in Q1 2026, suggesting some disillusionment with the pace of cuts.
- **Treasury Notes (2-10Y)** → duration/term premium bet. Still building. Asset managers adding longs through Mar 2026.
- **Eris SOFR Swaps** → swap spread/basis and pure rates directional. The newest and most concentrated expression of the trade. Growing explosively.
- **UST Bond (30Y)** → conspicuously absent from the theme. The market is not extending the rate-cut bet to the very long end.

The risk scenario is a coordinated unwind: if the SOFR-3M asset manager retreat (Q1 2026) spreads to Treasury notes and Eris, the concentrated Eris positions would be the most vulnerable to a disorderly exit.
